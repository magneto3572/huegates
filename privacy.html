Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026 · HueGates by Board Lantern

In short

HueGates is a puzzle game for Android and iOS, made by Board Lantern, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). The game has no account of its own and never asks for your name, email address or contacts. Your progress is saved on your device. Ads, anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports keep the game free and working, and you can control them in the game's Settings.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, hints, hammers, skins, streaks, event progress) and settings (sound, music, theme, language) are stored in the game's own storage on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them, unless you use cloud save.

Gameplay statistics and crash reports

We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to find levels that are too hard and to fix crashes. They collect:

  • gameplay events, such as starting, finishing or leaving a level, moves, stars, and use of hints and boosters;
  • an app-instance identifier created by Firebase, device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored);
  • technical details of a crash when the game crashes.

Firebase is set not to collect the advertising ID and not to use this data for ads. You can switch statistics and crash reports off at any time in Settings → Usage statistics.

Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: ads you choose to watch for a reward, and short ads between some levels. To show, measure and protect ads from fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking), IP address, device information and how you interact with ads.

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your choice through Google's consent tool before any ad loads. You can change it in Settings → Ad choices.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still show but are not personalised using the IDFA. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you buy "Remove ads", the ads between levels stop. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive only a confirmation that you own it. We never see your name, payment card or billing details. Their privacy policies apply to the payment.

Google Play Games (Android, optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game can save a copy of your progress in your Google account (cloud save) and, where available, show your best daily streak and Time Rush score on leaderboards under your Play Games profile name. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out or delete Play Games data in the Google Play Games settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your lives are full or a new daily puzzle is ready). They are created on your device and nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off in your device settings.

Who receives data

We do not sell your data. The data described above goes only to these service providers, which process it for the purposes stated:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, AdMob, Google Play Billing, Google Play Games), see the Google Privacy Policy;
  • Apple (App Store purchases and notifications on iOS), see the Apple Privacy Policy.

These providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game. Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months and Crashlytics keeps crash reports for up to 90 days. AdMob, Google Play and Apple keep data according to their own policies.

Children

HueGates is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and want their data removed, contact us.

Your choices and rights

  • Change ad consent in Settings → Ad choices, and tracking permission in iOS Settings.
  • Turn gameplay statistics and crash reports off in Settings → Usage statistics.
  • Sign out of Google Play Games or delete its data in Play Games settings.
  • Delete all data on your device by uninstalling the game.

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the game has no accounts, we usually cannot link data to you. Contact us with your request and we will help as far as we can, including asking our service providers to delete data linked to your app installation.

Security

Data is sent to our service providers over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, but we use reputable providers and collect as little as we can.
